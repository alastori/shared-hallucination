Can You Predict Your Last Good Year?

Content note: This episode discusses assisted dying, terminal illness, dementia, depression, suicide, and end-of-life care. Daniel Kahneman spent his career showing that humans are bad at predicting their own happiness. His final decision, later reported as assisted suicide in Switzerland, turns that same forecasting problem back on us: can a choice still be autonomous when the future self you are judging is only a guess?
