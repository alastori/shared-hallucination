What If We Were Reading AI Wrong?

A famous AI neuron seemed to respond to both cats and cars. Nick Cammarata proposes that it was detecting a shared shape. What would that change about how we study AI? The cast explains neurons, features, and circuits, then separates a promising research tool from proof of whole-system safety. The Goodfire account is a preliminary working hypothesis.
